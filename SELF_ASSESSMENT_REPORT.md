## What we did not manage

The wireframes were made as detailed mockups and picked up features outside our scope: saving jobs, user accounts, notifications, reporting suspicious postings, and a profile/CV panel. The CV panel directly contradicts "no CV storage" under *Not building*. We noticed this only while comparing the drawings with the proposal to fill in this table, close to the deadline.

The proposal also does not answer where the job postings come from (manual entry, open data, or a partner site's API), even though it rules out bulk scraping.

## What we would do differently

Draw the wireframes from the "Building" list in the Scope, and check every button in the drawings against the Screens section before polishing them. Write the AI-LOG entry each time we use an assistant instead of reconstructing it at the end.
