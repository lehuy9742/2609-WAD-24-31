## What we did not manage

Our mockups ended up with more than our first scope: saving jobs, student accounts, notifications, reporting suspicious postings, and a profile panel. With the deadline close, we chose to add these to the Scope in a limited form and fence them with *Not building* items (no CV upload, no email/SMS, no social login), instead of redrawing the screens. That makes the semester's work larger than we first planned.

## What we would do differently

Draw the wireframes from the "Building" list in the Scope, and check every button in the drawings against the Screens section before polishing them. Write the AI-LOG entry each time we use an assistant instead of reconstructing it at the end.
